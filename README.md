### Now playing

I build products that have to survive the living room.

Senior Frontend Engineer and Connected TV Lead. Ten years of shipping web, mobile, and Smart TV applications - including apps built from scratch for **Roku**, **Samsung Tizen**, **LG webOS**, and other HTML5 TV platforms. That includes 2017-era hardware, Tizen 3, and devices that do not forgive a careless bundle.

The interesting work is rarely the happy path. It is playback on constrained SoCs, hosted Tizen/webOS shells that keep native APIs without store-review lock-in, polyfills, and a modern React/TypeScript stack that still boots on a TV from almost a decade ago.

I lead teams the same way I ship players: architecture first, then the ugly platform details, then production.
